Layout and interaction rules
  • Page gutter: 16 px on mobile, 24 px or more on desktop. Content max width about 1120 px.
  • Cards: 16 px radius, 20-24 px padding, one soft shadow level only (none in dark mode).
  • Touch targets: at least 44 × 44 px, with at least 8 px between neighbors. Buttons and inputs are 48 px tall.
  • Focus: visible 3 px ring on every interactive element, never removed. Keyboard-only use must work.
  • Inputs: visible label above, 1.5 px border with at least 3:1 contrast, error text with icon linked to the field.
  • Motion: subtle and short; respect prefers-reduced-motion; nothing flashes.
