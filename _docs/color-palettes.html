BuzzQR color palette: A, Azul Confianza

Selected on 2026-11-14 for BuzzQR, a QR payment app for Mexico's financial sector (confidence, trust, innovation, security). Full rules, supporting tokens and typography are in BRAND.md. The two alternatives that were not chosen are kept below for reference.

Selected: A. Azul Confianza

Strengths

Watch out
